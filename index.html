Label and printer

Label size

Negative moves left or up, positive moves right or down. Set for your Knaon printer: −0.12 and +0.05. If a test label prints off to one side, change it by about 0.03 at a time.

How it will print

2 × 1 in · 203 dpi

Printing on the Mac

  1. After loading or changing a roll, hold the printer's feed button about 3 seconds until it beeps.
  2. Open the PDF in Preview and press Cmd+P, then Show Details.
  3. Paper Size: 2 × 1 in, margins 0 (Manage Custom Sizes the first time).
  4. Orientation: Portrait. Auto Rotate: off. Scale: 100%.
  5. Print page 1 as a test, then the rest. Save these settings as a Preset.
